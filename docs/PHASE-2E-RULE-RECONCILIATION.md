## Identity and operational registration

New registrations require both a permanent, globally unique application student ID and an internal UUID. These are different from any genuine pre-existing external student ID. The generated ID is legitimate new application identity, not a fabricated historical source ID. Propose immutable server-generated display IDs with a database unique constraint and collision-safe allocation; no prefix, numbering range, encoding or sequential format selected. Browser `max+1` is unsuitable for concurrent users. Resolve possible duplicates before creation through authorized review; matching by name may surface candidates but never automatically merges them. Whether/how verified legacy people receive display IDs needs explicit rollout approval. Never change IDs on centre/grade/shift transfer.

Confirmed registration fields map to children (UUID/generated ID), private_child_profiles (name and controlled gender, separately from raw source sex labels), enrolments/placement observations (centre, grade, shift, enrolled date, status, exit date), and a restricted nullable Class-10 board-result projection. Board result is Yes/No/unknown, not false by default. Its historical exam year/evidence and repeat/correction policy remain unspecified; do not invent an exam date or silently overwrite historical results. No new board-exam relation is assumed. Main challenge notes are present in the frontend but not part of the newly confirmed minimum; privacy/scope approval is still required.

## Attendance contract and edge cases

Use accepted, scope-eligible, date-filtered P/A/L counts: 100 × P/(P+A+L). L lowers the percentage by entering the denominator. WO/MM and other confirmed holidays never enter either count. Unrecorded dates remain unknown; missing marks must not enter the denominator. Non-scheduled holiday determination requires an authoritative calendar; a missing session row or missing child mark is not evidence of a holiday. Conflicting P/A/L on a confirmed holiday must be reviewed, not silently overridden or counted twice. Session delivery counts remain separate from this percentage.

For illustration only, synthetic P=3,A=1,L=1 gives 60%, irrespective of excluded WO/MM or unknown entries. Recommend NULL rate and “No recorded eligible attendance” for denominator zero, with separate unknown/excluded/conflict counts and coverage. This display, aggregate weighting and rounding remain review proposals, not additional confirmed policy. Recommend ratio of summed counts for group metrics, labelled explicitly; do not silently average child percentages. No dropout, engagement-band or minimum-attendance threshold is newly approved. Frontend H may only represent a confirmed holiday after a reviewed mapping; do not replace original WO/MM reasons with H.

## Academic periods and maximum-score conflict

Academic year starting April 1 of year Y ends March 31 of Y+1. Q1 Apr1–Jun30; Q2 Jul1–Sep30; Q3 Oct1–Dec31; Q4 Jan1–Mar31 of Y+1. Database definitions should use a consistent boundary convention (proposed half-open date ranges ending Apr1/Jul1/Oct1/Jan1 as appropriate); operational enrolment exit-date meaning remains separately unresolved. Policy does not establish the year of undated source rows. A verified full date may support year attribution under a separately reviewed transformation; quarter, tab proximity or filename alone may not.

E3 records morning maximum metadata 100, evening 25, and all 1,693 observed numeric results <=25 including 802 morning values. E1 confirms 25 for the current process and says 100 *may* be outdated. Keep the raw 100 metadata and literal scores immutable; flag affected definitions/results as scale conflicts. Do not divide by four, multiply, overwrite metadata, or treat <=25 alone as proof that a historical result used a 25-mark paper. Source/assessment owners must confirm affected historical scope with evidence before assigning approved 25 definitions and admitting those results to final operational tables/reporting. Until then, exclude the disputed results from final-scale comparisons and reconcile them as held exceptions. Zero remains a real score, missing is unknown.

Routine repeats are not required. Propose one accepted result per child/approved definition, corrected through attributable revisions. Remove routine attempt_number from the proposed current-process relation; do not synthesize attempt 1. Any genuinely evidenced exceptional repeat needs an explicit separate design decision. Existing frontend uses whole marks, while source validation permits decimals: current UI validation is verified, but universal historical decimal rejection is not approved. Q1–Q4 cannot yet be labelled F1–F4 by assumption. Sample year, performance bands and equal-max paper comparisons are not newly approved business policy.
